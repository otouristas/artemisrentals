Before you picture coastal scooter days, check two things: that your licence covers the engine size, and that you meet Artemis rental terms (riders aged 23 to 75, with a licence held for more than one year). Both get checked at the desk in Apollonia, and it is far better to know before you fly.

## The short answer

| Your licence | Can you rent our 125cc to 200cc scooters? |
| --- | --- |
| Motorcycle A1, A2 or A | Yes |
| Car only (category B) | No |
| AM (mopeds up to 50cc) | No |
| Non-EU motorcycle licence | Yes, with an International Driving Permit that shows the motorcycle category |

## Why a car licence is not enough

In some countries a car licence lets you ride a 125cc scooter at home. Greece does not extend that to visitors. To ride anything above 50cc here you need a motorcycle category on your licence, and our whole scooter fleet is 125cc or larger. If you are stopped by the police without the right category, the fine is yours, and riding without the right category can complicate any insurance claim after an accident.

## Which scooter class

Artemis offers SYM Symphony models from 125cc to 200cc plus Fiddle and JET4 125 options. Choose engine size based on hills, passenger load and your experience, not only the daily rate.

## Choosing cc for two riders

125cc models handle solo riders and short hills well. With a passenger, consider the Symphony 150 or 200 for the climbs from the coast back up to Apollonia. The 125s suit confident riders on shorter hops, such as Apollonia to Kastro or to Faros.

## Non-EU licences

If your licence was issued outside the EU or EEA, carry an International Driving Permit alongside it, and make sure the permit lists the motorcycle category. Without that category on the permit, a home motorcycle licence can be hard to prove at a roadside check.

When in doubt, send us a photo of both sides of your licence before you travel. It takes a minute and saves disappointment at the desk.

## Pickup and season

Scooters are collected and returned at the **Apollonia office only**. We cannot deliver them to Kamares port, so plan to reach Apollonia by bus or taxi first. Published scooter rates cover **11 June to 10 September**, from €30 to €40 a day depending on model and season. See the [rates page](/en/rates).

## Road manners

Wear a helmet on every ride, avoid night rides on day one, and do not treat island roads like a closed track. The goal is beach time, not adrenaline. Read [driving in Sifnos](/en/blog/driving-in-sifnos) for road character and parking.
