We rent both, so we have no reason to push you one way. After decades of watching guests come back from beach days on Sifnos, the pattern is clear: a scooter is wonderful for the right people on the right days, and a car is the safer default for almost everyone else.

## The quick decision

| Your situation | Our suggestion |
| --- | --- |
| You only have a car licence | Car. A car licence does not cover a 125cc scooter in Greece. |
| Three or more people | Car |
| Children, or a child seat | Car |
| Ferry luggage and no hotel transfer | Car (free pickup at the port) |
| July or August, exposed beaches in the north | Car |
| Two people, light bags, motorcycle licence, settled weather | Scooter works well |
| You want to park right by the beach in August | Scooter is easier to park |
| You want to collect at the ferry | Car (scooters are Apollonia office only) |

## Licence: the deciding factor for many

This rules out more scooter plans than anything else. Our scooters are 125cc to 200cc, and in Greece those need a **motorcycle licence, category A1 or higher**. A car licence is not enough, even if it lets you ride a 125 at home. If you have the right licence, read the [scooter licence rules](/en/blog/scooter-licence-rules-sifnos) for the detail on non-EU licences.

## Luggage and the ferry

Every visitor arrives at Kamares port with bags. With a car, we meet the ferry: the car is waiting at the quay at no extra charge, and you drive straight to your hotel. A scooter has to be collected at our Apollonia office, about 5.5 km uphill, so you first need a bus, a taxi or a hotel transfer.

## Wind, heat and hills

Sifnos sits on a ridge. Apollonia is high in the middle, every beach is down at sea level, and every beach day ends with a climb back up. On a 125cc with two people and beach bags, that climb is slow.

From mid-July through August the **meltemi**, a strong northerly wind, blows across the island on many afternoons. On exposed stretches, especially the long road north to Cherronisos, crosswinds on two wheels are tiring and occasionally unnerving. In a car it is a non-event, and you can simply drive to a sheltered bay like Vathi.

The sun matters too. At 2 p.m. in August, a car with air conditioning and a parasol in the boot is a very different experience from a scooter seat that has been parked in the sun.

## Parking

Here the scooter wins. In August, Platys Gialos, Vathi and Chrysopigi fill up by late morning, and a scooter slips into spaces a car never could. In Apollonia and Kastro, where cars park at the edge and walk in, a scooter gets you a little closer.

With a car, the answer is timing: arrive before 10 a.m. or after 4 p.m. Our [driving guide](/en/blog/driving-in-sifnos) has parking notes for each village and beach.

Compared with the cheapest car, a scooter saves roughly €15 to €30 a day in summer. A car carries up to five, so for a family or a group of friends it is cheaper per person. Published scooter rates only cover 11 June to 10 September; outside those dates, a small manual car is the budget option. Full tables are on the [rates page](/en/rates).

## Safety, plainly

Island roads are narrow, winding and often have no barrier. Gravel collects at the edge of beach roads. Nobody needs to be nervous, but a scooter forgives fewer mistakes than a car. If you have not ridden for years, Sifnos in August is not the place to start again. Helmets are included with every scooter, and they go on for every ride.

## Our honest recommendation

- **Most visitors:** a small car. Free port pickup, luggage space, shade, and no worries about wind.
- **Couples with a motorcycle licence who ride regularly at home:** a scooter for a few days in settled weather is a lovely way to see the island.
- **Not sure?** Book the car. You will not regret having a roof on a windy afternoon.
