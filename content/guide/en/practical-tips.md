## Timing beats everything

In peak summer the difference between a calm Platys Gialos morning and a stressful noon is about **90 minutes**. Leave Apollonia early; take long lunches in the shade; swim again after 16:00.

## Wind (meltemi)

Northerlies are common in July–August. If whitecaps show on open coasts, pivot to **Vathi**, **Kamares**, or sheltered Faros pockets. Scooter riders should treat crosswinds seriously on exposed ridges.

## Parking manners

- Never block chapels, bins, or hotel gates
- Fold mirrors in tight lanes
- On dirt finales (e.g. toward Vroulidia), park without ripping soft verges

## Heat & hydration

Island asphalt radiates. Keep water in the car, never leave children/pets in parked vehicles, and prefer morning hikes.

## Scooter-specific

Helmets on, no racing the bus, watch gravel near beaches, and avoid two-up overloads. A 125cc scooter needs a motorcycle licence (A1 or higher), not just a car licence: see [scooter licence rules](/en/blog/scooter-licence-rules-sifnos). If unsure, choose a car; [car or scooter on Sifnos](/en/blog/car-or-scooter-sifnos) compares the two.

## Security basics

Hide bags, skip glove-box theatres of wealth, and take passports to dinner in busy centres if your hotel safe is limited.

## Roads in one paragraph

Roads radiate from Apollonia to the coast rather than circling the island, so most days are out-and-back. The main roads are sealed but narrow, winding and often without barriers. Sound the horn lightly before blind bends, give way where the road pinches on a climb, and expect the last stretch to a few coves (Vroulidia, for example) to be rough. [Driving in Sifnos](/en/blog/driving-in-sifnos) has the full picture, including parking village by village.

## Villages: park once, then walk

- **Apollonia:** the centre is narrow and its main lane is pedestrian. Park at the edge once for the evening.
- **Kastro:** no driving inside the medieval village. Park outside and walk in.
- **Artemonas:** quiet residential lanes; use the edge of the village and keep gates clear.
- **Kamares:** never block the quay access road, especially around ferry times.

## Fuel

Petrol stations are few and spread out. Fill up before a full day to Cherronisos or a long beach loop, and ask us at handover where the nearest pump is. Return the car with the same fuel level as at pickup unless we agreed otherwise.

## Documents

Bring your physical driving licence and a passport or ID card. If your licence was issued outside the EU or EEA, bring an International Driving Permit as well. Drivers must be 23 to 75 with a licence held for more than a year.
