## A local desk, not a call centre

If you are searching to [rent a car in Sifnos](/en) or comparing Sifnos car rental, you want three things: a maintained vehicle, clear pickup at the ferry, and a human reply. Artemis Rental has done that from Apollonia since **1988**, as a family business. When you message us, the person who answers is the person who hands over the keys.

## Free pickup points for cars

- **Kamares port** (ferry arrival)
- Heliport
- Apollonia office
- Artemonas
- Kastro

Vathi, Platys Gialos, Faros, Chrysopigi, and Cherronisos are available on request (fee confirmed when we reply). Returns work the same way, so you can drop the car at the port before your ferry home.

## What is included

- Third-party liability insurance
- Regular inspection and maintenance on the island
- Free delivery and return at the five points above
- Local help: which beach suits the wind, where to park, what to do when the ferry is late

Excess, optional extra cover and the damage procedure are explained at pickup. If you want them in writing before you travel, ask when you enquire.

## Who can rent

- Drivers aged **23 to 75**
- A valid licence held for **more than one year**
- Licences from outside the EU or EEA: bring an International Driving Permit as well

The details, including what to bring to the desk, are in [what you need to rent a car on Sifnos](/en/blog/sifnos-car-rental-requirements).

## How booking works

1. Browse the [car fleet](/en/cars) and [seasonal rates](/en/rates)

For July and August, enquire as soon as your ferry is booked. Automatics go first.
