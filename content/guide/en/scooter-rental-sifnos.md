A scooter is the classic way to see a Greek island, and on Sifnos it works well for the right people: one or two riders, light bags, settled weather. We have rented two-wheelers from the same Apollonia office since 1988, so this page is honest about when a scooter is a joy and when a car is the better call.

## How to book a scooter

1. Choose a model on the [scooter fleet](/en/scooters) page, or let us suggest one for the number of riders and the hills you plan to climb.
2. [Send your dates](/en/book) through the booking form or WhatsApp. There is no prepayment to reserve.
3. We confirm availability personally, then you collect the scooter at our Apollonia office.

Have your motorcycle licence ready to check (see below). If you would rather be met at the ferry, [cars](/en/cars) can be delivered to Kamares port free of charge.

## Where you collect it

Scooters are handed over and returned at our **Apollonia office only**. Unlike our cars, we cannot deliver scooters to Kamares port. From the ferry, take the bus or a taxi up to Apollonia (about 5.5 km, ten minutes by road) and collect the scooter there.

## The licence you need

This is the question we answer most, so here it is plainly:

- A **125cc scooter needs a motorcycle licence**: category A1, A2 or A.
- A **car-only licence (category B) is not enough** for a 125cc scooter in Greece, whatever the rules are at home.
- The **AM category** covers mopeds up to 50cc only, so it does not cover our fleet.
- Riders must be **23 to 75** and have held their licence for more than a year, the same terms as our cars.

If you are unsure what your licence covers, send us a photo of both sides before you travel. The full explanation is in our [scooter licence rules](/en/blog/scooter-licence-rules-sifnos) article.

## When a scooter suits Sifnos

- Couples or solo travellers with light bags
- Short hops between Apollonia, Artemonas, Kastro, Faros and Platys Gialos
- Riders already confident on hills, hairpins and the odd gravel patch
- Calm days, or days you are happy to choose a sheltered beach

## When a car is the better choice

- Families, or anyone carrying ferry luggage
- July and August afternoons when the **meltemi** wind is up: exposed ridges are hard work on two wheels
- Long runs to Cherronisos and Vroulidia in the north
- Drivers who want to collect at the port the moment they land

Read [car or scooter on Sifnos](/en/blog/car-or-scooter-sifnos) for the full comparison, or the [getting around guide](/en/sifnos-guide/getting-around) for buses and taxis too.

## Riding well on the island

Wear the helmet on every ride, short ones included. Keep speeds low on blind bends, watch for loose gravel at the edge of beach roads, and do not load two people and beach gear onto a 125cc for a steep climb. The [practical driving tips](/en/sifnos-guide/practical-tips) cover wind, heat and parking.
